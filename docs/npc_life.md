# NPC life

> Status: notes to think about later, nothing decided. Far future, not part of any milestone yet.

Today NPCs are routines: a miner shuttles between the field and its station forever, a trader
crawls from gate to station, a garrison fighter sits docked until an invasion. They have no reason
to do what they do and nothing carries over: a destroyed miner is just gone.

## Questions

- **Why do NPCs live?** What does an NPC want: money, safety, a better ship, revenge,
  reputation with a faction? Routines would follow from goals instead of being the goal.
- **Money.** Can NPCs save and spend credits (the one galactic currency, see `concept.md`
  "Money")? Earn by mining, trading, bounties; spend on repairs, fuel, ships, equipment.
- **Consequences of being poor.** A broke fighter may not be able to repair after a battle and
  has to fly damaged, do cheap work or sell its ship. Right now NPC ships repair for free when
  they dock (`Ship.free_repairs`), only because they have no money yet.
- **Names and identity.** Generated names, so an NPC is a person and not "Miner 3". The same
  pilot could change ships and keep the name, the credits and the history.
- **Pilots living their own lives**, as in Space Rangers: careers, choices, rising and falling,
  and the player meeting the same pilots again (an ally from the last invasion, a pirate who
  escaped).
- **A-Life (S.T.A.L.K.E.R.).** The world goes on off-screen: NPCs move, trade, fight and die
  whether or not the player is there. Ties into "Simulation is separate from presentation" in
  `concept.md` and the open question of off-screen simulation of other systems.
- **Pillar "Same rules for everyone".** Whatever the player can do with money, ships and
  pods (repairs, upgrades, changing ships, ejecting), an NPC should eventually be able to do too.
